What We Do

Market entry, business development, and capital access.

We provide on-the-ground commercial support to companies pursuing opportunities across the GCC and MENA. Each engagement begins with a clear set of objectives—such as identifying a suitable partner, pursuing a contract, establishing a local presence, or connecting a project with potential funding sources.

01

Market Entry and Establishment Support

We assess your fit for the region and help you plan your market entry. In Qatar, this may include coordinating information on establishment through the Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MOCI), Qatar Free Zones, or the Qatar Financial Centre (QFC), as well as licensing, ownership structures, and operational requirements. Where legal, tax, or regulatory advice is required, we work alongside appropriately qualified advisers.

02

Business Development and Partner Sourcing

We identify and assess potential partners, distributors, agents, and customers, then facilitate relevant introductions. Our network includes contacts across public-sector organisations, EPC contractors, industrial buyers, and private-sector companies.

03

Supplier and Vendor Representation

For equipment and materials suppliers, we help present your offering to relevant buyers and support activities such as pre-qualification, tender participation, and commercial discussions.

04

Bid and Tender Support

We help you understand procurement processes, organise bid preparation, and coordinate with relevant local stakeholders. Our support is designed to help you navigate each stage of the process in a professional and compliant manner.

05

Investor Introductions and Project Funding Support

For project owners and technology companies, we help identify potential investors, family offices, and other funding sources in the region. We can support preparation and introductions throughout the process; any investment decision remains with the relevant funding parties.

06

Ongoing Commercial Representation

For companies without a regional office, we can provide ongoing commercial support in the market—maintaining business relationships, following up on opportunities, and helping keep your company visible to relevant stakeholders.
