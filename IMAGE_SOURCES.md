## Homepage background carousel

The homepage automatically cycles through West Bay, The Pearl architecture, and UDC Tower, with a four-second hold followed by a three-second crossfade. Optimized desktop and mobile WebP versions are served locally; the original existing JPEGs are preserved. The banner has no playback controls, location captions, or photography links, as requested by the owner on 16 November 2026.

- **West Bay:** the Pexels photograph credited above, exported as `images/west-bay-background.webp` (1600 × 1067) and `images/west-bay-mobile.webp` (900 × 600).
- **The Pearl architecture:** Abdul Raheem Kannath, Viva Bahriya lagoon and residential towers, Pearl Qatar, Doha. Source: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-large-body-of-water-surrounded-by-tall-buildings-IVoxetBHEX4 . Original: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1728508494010-5a08abcf922f?auto=format&fit=max&w=2400&q=90 . Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license (commercial use and modifications permitted; public attribution not required). Resized and recompressed as `images/the-pearl-background.webp` (1920 × 1280) and `images/the-pearl-mobile.webp` (900 × 600), with display cropping via object-fit. This architecture photo replaces the proposed yacht-marina photograph; the CC BY marina photo is not included in the website.
- **UDC Tower:** the existing permitted official photograph credited above, exported as `images/udc-tower-background.webp` (1200 × 800) and `images/udc-tower-mobile.webp` (900 × 600).

Reduced-motion preferences keep a static image. The outgoing photo stays opaque underneath the incoming fade to avoid dark flashes. Rotation stops while the page is hidden or the banner is offscreen.
