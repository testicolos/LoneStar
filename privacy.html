How information provided through this website’s enquiry form is used.

Contacting us

The enquiry form prepares an email in your own email app, addressed to info@lonestaralserdal.com. You review and send that email yourself. Submitting the form does not by itself send your enquiry to us.

Information you choose to share

Your enquiry may include your name, company, email address, phone number, country, industry, business objective, and message. We use the information you send to respond to your enquiry and discuss the support you are seeking.

Questions about your information

If you have a question about information you have shared with us, please contact info@lonestaralserdal.com.
