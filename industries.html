Industries We Serve

Industries we serve.

We work across sectors that are central to the Gulf economy, supporting both project owners and the businesses that serve them.

01

Oil and Gas

We work with service providers, equipment and technology suppliers seeking to develop relationships with operators, EPC contractors, and other supply-chain participants in Qatar and the wider GCC.

02

Construction and Infrastructure

From materials suppliers to specialist contractors, we support companies exploring market entry and business development opportunities across the region’s construction and infrastructure sectors.

03

Technology

We help technology companies assess their regional opportunity, develop their positioning, and explore potential pilots and growth pathways. This can include introductions to enterprise buyers, relevant public-sector initiatives, and potential investors.

04

Fundraising and Project Finance

We support project owners and growing companies seeking to engage with regional investors, family offices, and other potential funding partners.

05

Equipment and Materials Supply

We represent suppliers seeking to connect with regional buyers and can support pre-qualification, tender participation, and commercial discussions.

06

Adjacent Sectors

Our network and approach may also extend to adjacent sectors, including logistics, industrial services, healthcare, and education, where there is a clear fit between your offering and regional demand.
